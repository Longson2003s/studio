# **App Name**: ClinicWise AI

## Core Features:

- Secure User Authentication & Profiles: Allows doctors to securely log in and manage their professional profiles, ensuring data privacy and access control.
- Structured Patient Data Input: A comprehensive, form-based interface for medical professionals to input patient demographics, presenting symptoms, medical history, examination findings, and lab results.
- AI-powered Differential Diagnosis Tool: A generative AI tool that processes input patient data to provide a ranked list of probable differential diagnoses, complete with estimated probabilities, highlighting key assumptions made due to missing or unclear data.
- AI Diagnostic Recommendation Tool: An AI tool that suggests relevant follow-up investigations, including laboratory tests, imaging studies, and specialist consultations, aligned with clinical guidelines based on the generated differential diagnoses.
- AI-generated Treatment Protocol Tool: A generative AI tool that recommends evidence-based treatment protocols, outlining medication suggestions (including dosages and administration), non-pharmacological interventions, and potential contraindications, referencing established guidelines.
- Real-time Risk & Warning System: Identifies and displays critical risk factors, potential drug-drug interactions, and medication alerts based on patient information and suggested treatment plans, drawing from a comprehensive medical knowledge base.
- Integrated Guideline Reference Library: Provides doctors with quick access to and citations for relevant medical guidelines, offering direct links or snippets to support suggested diagnoses, investigations, and treatment protocols.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary brand color: A professional and trustworthy mid-tone blue (#2E7ACC). This color signifies reliability and calm, suitable for a medical context. Hue is 210, saturation 60%, lightness 45%.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated variant of the primary hue (#DEE6ED), providing a clean, airy, and professional canvas. Hue is 210, saturation 20%, lightness 92%.
- Accent color: A vibrant yet professional cyan-blue (#26CFCF) for critical calls-to-action, alerts, and highlighting important information, ensuring strong contrast against the primary and background colors. Hue is 180, saturation 70%, lightness 50%.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for its modern, clear, and tech-forward feel, emphasizing the advanced nature of the AI-powered CDSS. Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for excellent readability and neutrality, suitable for presenting complex medical information.
- Use a set of clear, minimalist, line-based icons throughout the application. Icons should be easily understandable and support quick visual recognition of actions and data points without being distracting.
- Implement a clean, organized, and modular layout with ample white space. Content should be clearly segmented into logical blocks (e.g., Patient Summary, Differential Diagnosis, Treatment Plan), prioritizing scannability and ease of navigation for busy medical professionals. The design must be fully responsive for desktop and tablet usage.
- Incorporate subtle and functional animations for user feedback, such as loading indicators for AI processes, smooth transitions between different patient data sections, and mild hover effects on interactive elements, ensuring a responsive and pleasant user experience without unnecessary flamboyance.
